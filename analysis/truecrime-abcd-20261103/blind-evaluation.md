## 2. SCORING RUBRIC (100-POINT SYSTEM)

1. **Hook Quality (0–25):** Immediate scroll-stopping power, curiosity gap, lack of dead air, lack of premature spoilers.
2. **Story Clarity & Premise (0–20):** How easily the audience understands what is happening, who the characters are, and the stakes.
3. **Causal Escalation (0–20):** Forward momentum where each beat causes the next; absence of random time-skips or unmotivated actions.
4. **Payoff & Resolution (0–20):** Climax strength, satisfaction of open loops, emotional/comedic payoff, clean exit.
5. **Absence of Repetition & Filler (0–15):** Efficiency of footage, absence of redundant dialogue/driving shots, respect for mobile viewer attention.

## 5. KEY BLIND EVALUATION INSIGHTS

1. **The Automatic Hook Contract is actively destructive:** Candidate RED scored 31 because the pipeline forced a hook anchor that spoiled the arrest in second 3. Candidate GREEN (identical in every way except removing the forced anchor) scored 67 (+36 points).
2. **Single High-Density Shorts dominate Serialized Trilogies:** Both Candidate BLUE (94) and Candidate GOLD (96) recognized that a 9-minute raw video with 3 minutes of confrontation must be concentrated into a single 60–80s punchy short. Splitting it into 3 parts forces 70% filler and kills viral velocity.
3. **Media-Grounded Editorial Architecture (GOLD) edges out Unstructured Generation (BLUE):** While BLUE achieved 94 points through natural intelligence, GOLD achieved 96 points because its formal open loop tracking and information budgeting eliminated all residual ambiguities.
